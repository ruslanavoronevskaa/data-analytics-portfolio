### 📊 SaaS Revenue & Customer Metrics Dashboard

**Business Question**

How do subscription metrics change over time, and what factors influence revenue growth and customer retention?

**Highlights**

- Calculated SaaS metrics (MRR, ARPPU, New MRR, Expansion/Contraction MRR, LT, LTV)
- Built an interactive Tableau dashboard
- Analyzed revenue growth, churn, customer lifetime, and retention trends

**Tools**

SQL • PostgreSQL • Tableau
